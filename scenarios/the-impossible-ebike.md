# The Impossible E-Bike

Two participants, two Fos, one used e-bike.

- **Maya** (seller) is played by ________
- **Dev** (buyer) is played by ________

Each participant sees the listing and **only their own profile**.

---

## The listing (public)

> **FOR SALE: 2023 Rad Power RadCity 5 Plus e-bike** (step-thru, grey), ~800 miles.
> Always stored indoors, new brake pads in August. Charger included.
> **$1,400.** Pickup in the Mission, SF.

---

## Organizer's answer key (participants don't see this)

- **Price alone:** Dev's maximum ($950) is below Maya's minimum ($1,050). No price works.
- **Bundles:** every workable deal includes the **sofa**, which forces a **Saturday
  10am-2pm** pickup (the only time Dev has the truck). With the sofa, desk and hook
  included, any cash price from **$785 to $950** works for both sides.
- **Lock and helmet** are worth about the same to each side, so they move value
  between the two without adding to the total.
- **Price-only control:** remove the furniture from Maya's profile and Dev's
  furniture values. No deal exists.
